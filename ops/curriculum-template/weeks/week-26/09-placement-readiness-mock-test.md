Q: A shirt marked at ₹800 is sold at a 15% discount. What is the selling price?
A) ₹640
*B) ₹680
C) ₹700
D) ₹720
Explanation: 15% of 800 is 120, so the selling price is 800 - 120 = ₹680.

Q: The price of sugar rises by 25%. By what percentage must a family reduce its consumption to keep its spending unchanged?
A) 25%
*B) 20%
C) 15%
D) 33.33%
Explanation: Reduction = 25 / (100 + 25) × 100 = 20%.

Q: An article bought for ₹400 is sold for ₹460. What is the profit percentage?
A) 12%
B) 13.04%
*C) 15%
D) 60%
Explanation: Profit = 60; profit % = 60 / 400 × 100 = 15%.

Q: If A : B = 3 : 5 and B : C = 2 : 3, what is A : B : C?
A) 3 : 5 : 3
*B) 6 : 10 : 15
C) 3 : 10 : 15
D) 6 : 5 : 9
Explanation: Make B equal: A : B = 6 : 10 and B : C = 10 : 15, so A : B : C = 6 : 10 : 15.

Q: A can finish a job in 12 days and B in 18 days. Working together, how long do they take?
A) 6 days
*B) 7.2 days
C) 7.5 days
D) 15 days
Explanation: Combined rate = 1/12 + 1/18 = 5/36 of the job per day, so 36/5 = 7.2 days.

Q: A train 240 m long runs at 72 km/h. How long does it take to pass a pole?
A) 10 seconds
*B) 12 seconds
C) 15 seconds
D) 20 seconds
Explanation: 72 km/h = 72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s; time = 240 / 20 = 12 seconds.

Q: What is the simple interest on ₹5,000 at 8% per annum for 3 years?
A) ₹1,000
*B) ₹1,200
C) ₹1,250
D) ₹1,500
Explanation: SI = P × R × T / 100 = 5000 × 8 × 3 / 100 = ₹1,200.

Q: What is the compound interest on ₹10,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years, compounded annually?
A) ₹2,000
*B) ₹2,100
C) ₹2,200
D) ₹12,100
Explanation: Amount = 10000 × 1.1 × 1.1 = 12,100, so the interest is ₹2,100. ₹12,100 is the amount, not the interest.

Q: The average of five numbers is 24. When one number is removed, the average of the remaining four is 22. Which number was removed?
A) 2
B) 24
*C) 32
D) 46
Explanation: Total of five = 120; total of four = 88; removed number = 120 - 88 = 32.

Q: Two fair dice are thrown. What is the probability that the sum is 7?
*A) 1/6
B) 1/9
C) 7/36
D) 1/12
Explanation: Six of the 36 outcomes sum to 7: (1,6), (2,5), (3,4), (4,3), (5,2), (6,1). 6/36 = 1/6.

Q: In how many different ways can the letters of the word LEVEL be arranged?
A) 120
B) 60
*C) 30
D) 20
Explanation: 5 letters with L and E each repeated twice: 5! / (2! × 2!) = 120 / 4 = 30.

Q: A 40-litre mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 3 : 1. How much water must be added to make the ratio 3 : 2?
A) 5 litres
*B) 10 litres
C) 15 litres
D) 20 litres
Explanation: Milk = 30 L, water = 10 L. For 30 : (10 + x) = 3 : 2, 10 + x = 20, so x = 10 litres.

Q: Find the next number in the series 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
A) 40
*B) 42
C) 44
D) 36
Explanation: The differences are 4, 6, 8, 10, so the next difference is 12: 30 + 12 = 42.

Q: Find the next letter in the series A, C, F, J, O, ?
A) S
B) T
*C) U
D) V
Explanation: The positions increase by 2, 3, 4, 5, 6: O is 15, and 15 + 6 = 21, which is U.

Q: If CAT is coded as DBU, how is DOG coded in the same language?
*A) EPH
B) CNF
C) EPG
D) FQI
Explanation: Each letter moves one place forward: D to E, O to P, G to H.

Q: Pointing to a man, Riya says, "He is the son of my mother's only brother." How is the man related to Riya?
A) Brother
B) Uncle
*C) Cousin
D) Nephew
Explanation: Her mother's only brother is her maternal uncle, and his son is her cousin.

Q: Ravi walks 5 km north, turns right and walks 3 km, then turns right again and walks 5 km. Where is he now relative to his starting point?
A) 3 km west
*B) 3 km east
C) 5 km south
D) 8 km east
Explanation: North 5, then east 3, then south 5 brings him back level with the start, 3 km to the east.

Q: Statements: All pens are books. Some books are bags. Conclusions: I. Some pens are bags. II. Some bags are books. Which conclusion follows?
A) Only I
*B) Only II
C) Both I and II
D) Neither I nor II
Explanation: II is the reverse of "some books are bags", so it always follows. The pens need not overlap with the bags, so I does not follow.

Q: Which number does not belong in the group 9, 25, 49, 64, 121?
A) 9
B) 49
*C) 64
D) 121
Explanation: 9, 25, 49 and 121 are squares of the primes 3, 5, 7 and 11. 64 is 8 squared, and 8 is not prime.

Q: What is the angle between the hour and minute hands of a clock at 3:30?
A) 90°
*B) 75°
C) 60°
D) 105°
Explanation: At 3:30 the hour hand is at 90 + 15 = 105° and the minute hand at 180°. The difference is 75°.

Q: A non-leap year begins on a Wednesday. On which day does the next year begin?
A) Wednesday
*B) Thursday
C) Friday
D) Tuesday
Explanation: 365 days is 52 weeks plus 1 day, so the next year starts one day later.

Q: Five friends A, B, C, D and E sit in a row facing north. C is in the middle, A is at the left end, B sits immediately to the right of C, and E is not next to A. Who sits next to A?
A) B
B) C
*C) D
D) E
Explanation: Seats 1 to 5: A = 1, C = 3, B = 4. E cannot be in seat 2 next to A, so E = 5 and D = 2.

Q: Is x > 0? Statement 1: x² = 16. Statement 2: x³ = 64. Which is true?
A) Statement 1 alone is sufficient
*B) Statement 2 alone is sufficient, but statement 1 alone is not
C) Both statements together are needed
D) Neither statement is sufficient
Explanation: From statement 1, x could be 4 or -4. From statement 2, x must be 4, so x > 0.

Q: Choose the word closest in meaning to METICULOUS.
A) Careless
*B) Thorough
C) Hasty
D) Generous
Explanation: Meticulous means showing great attention to detail.

Q: Choose the word most nearly opposite in meaning to ABUNDANT.
A) Plentiful
*B) Scarce
C) Ample
D) Large
Explanation: Abundant means plentiful; scarce means in short supply.

Q: Which sentence is grammatically correct?
A) Neither the manager nor the employees was present.
*B) Neither the manager nor the employees were present.
C) Neither the manager or the employees were present.
D) Neither the manager nor the employees is present.
Explanation: With neither...nor, the verb agrees with the nearer subject ("employees", plural), and neither pairs with nor.

Q: Fill in the blank: She has been working at this company ___ 2022.
A) for
*B) since
C) from
D) by
Explanation: Since is used with a point in time; for is used with a duration (for four years).

Q: Which sentence is correct?
A) Each of the students have submitted their assignment.
*B) Each of the students has submitted the assignment.
C) Each of the students have submitted the assignments.
D) Each of students has submitted the assignment.
Explanation: Each is singular, so it takes has; option D also drops the article "the".

Q: What does the idiom "to burn the midnight oil" mean?
A) To waste resources
*B) To work or study late into the night
C) To start a quarrel
D) To celebrate a success
Explanation: The idiom refers to working late, originally by lamplight.

Q: Which word is spelt correctly?
A) Accomodate
*B) Accommodate
C) Acommodate
D) Acomodate
Explanation: Accommodate has a double c and a double m.

Q: Read the passage and answer. "Many students practise coding problems only by reading solutions. Research on learning suggests that recalling and applying an idea, rather than rereading it, makes it last longer. Solving a problem again from memory a week later is therefore more useful than reading ten new solutions." Which statement is best supported by the passage?
A) Reading solutions is useless and should be avoided entirely
*B) Practising recall, such as re-solving problems from memory, helps learning last
C) Students should solve ten new problems every day
D) Coding problems cannot be learned from solutions at all
Explanation: The passage favours recall and application over rereading; options A and D are more extreme than the passage, and C is not mentioned.

Q: Which OOP feature allows a subclass to provide its own version of a method defined in its parent class, chosen at runtime?
A) Encapsulation
*B) Method overriding (runtime polymorphism)
C) Method overloading
D) Abstraction through interfaces only
Explanation: Overriding replaces an inherited method, and the version called depends on the object's actual class at runtime.

Q: Which statement best describes encapsulation?
*A) Bundling data with the methods that operate on it and restricting direct access to the data
B) Creating many objects from one class
C) Inheriting properties from more than one parent class
D) Writing code that runs on many platforms
Explanation: Encapsulation hides internal state behind a controlled interface such as methods or properties.

Q: A table is in 1NF, and one non-key column depends on only part of a composite primary key. Moving that column into its own table achieves which normal form?
A) 1NF
*B) 2NF
C) 3NF
D) BCNF
Explanation: 2NF removes partial dependencies on part of a composite key; 3NF then removes transitive dependencies.

Q: A table has 5 rows, and its email column is NULL in 2 of them. What do COUNT(*) and COUNT(email) return?
A) 5 and 5
*B) 5 and 3
C) 3 and 3
D) 5 and 2
Explanation: COUNT(*) counts rows; COUNT(column) ignores NULL values.

Q: Which are necessary conditions for a deadlock? (Select all that apply.)
*A) Mutual exclusion
*B) Hold and wait
*C) Circular wait
D) Round-robin scheduling
Explanation: The four conditions are mutual exclusion, hold and wait, no preemption and circular wait. A scheduling policy is not one of them.

Q: Which statement about processes and threads is true?
A) Threads of the same process each have their own separate address space
*B) Threads of the same process share the process's memory, such as code and heap
C) A process cannot contain more than one thread
D) Switching between processes is always cheaper than switching between threads
Explanation: Threads share their process's address space but have their own stack and registers, which makes them lighter than processes.

Q: Which statements about TCP and UDP are correct? (Select all that apply.)
*A) TCP is connection-oriented and retransmits lost data
*B) UDP does not guarantee delivery or ordering
C) UDP sets up a three-way handshake before sending data
D) TCP is always faster than UDP
Explanation: The three-way handshake belongs to TCP. UDP has less overhead, so it is usually faster.

Q: What is the time complexity of binary search on a sorted array of n elements?
A) O(n)
*B) O(log n)
C) O(n log n)
D) O(1)
Explanation: Each comparison halves the remaining search space.

Q: Which statements about complexity are correct? (Select all that apply.)
*A) Quick sort is O(n²) in the worst case
*B) Hash map lookup is O(1) on average
*C) BFS on a graph with V vertices and E edges is O(V + E)
D) Merge sort is O(n²) in the worst case
Explanation: Merge sort is O(n log n) in every case. The other three statements are correct.
