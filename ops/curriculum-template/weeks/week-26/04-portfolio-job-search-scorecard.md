# Portfolio finalisation, job search strategy and the Placement Readiness Scorecard

The programme ends this week, but your placement season does not. This session makes sure everything a recruiter might
look at is finished, and gives you a system for the search ahead.

## Résumé v3 (final)

Start from v2 (Week 23) and the tutor's comments. Version 3 must:

- [ ] fit **one page**, single column, standard fonts, no tables or images in the text;
- [ ] follow the order contact → education → skills → projects → experience/internships → certifications →
      achievements;
- [ ] list the **finished** capstone first under projects, with the live URL, and your own contribution;
- [ ] use the bullet formula *action verb + what + technology + measurable result* for every project;
- [ ] contain only true, verifiable claims, and only skills you can be questioned on;
- [ ] be exported as `Firstname_Lastname_Resume.pdf` and approved by your tutor.

**Tailor it per role.** Keep one master résumé, then make small variants: a web-focused version leads with the capstone
and MP3; a data-focused version leads with MP4 and the data parts of your capstone. Change the order and emphasis, never
the facts.

## Profiles checklist

| Platform | Must have |
|---|---|
| **GitHub** | Profile README with bio, skills and links · **6 pinned repositories** (5 mini projects + capstone) · every pinned repo has a README with screenshots and a live link where applicable · regular contribution history |
| **LinkedIn** | Professional photo and banner · headline with target role + key skills · 3–5 line About section · Projects and Featured sections linked to live demos · skills aligned with the résumé · at least 3 project posts over the programme |
| **Naukri** | Résumé headline and profile summary · key skills matching target job descriptions · latest résumé uploaded · job alerts for 2–3 target roles and locations · profile updated regularly |
| **Portfolio site** | Live on GitHub Pages or similar · project cards with stack, links and demo videos · contact section and résumé download |

A quick test: open each profile in a private browser window, as a stranger would see it. Are the links working? Does
the first screen say who you are and what you build?

## The job application tracker

Applying without tracking leads to missed follow-ups and duplicated effort. Create a spreadsheet with these columns:

| Column | Example |
|---|---|
| Company | (company name) |
| Role | Graduate Software Engineer |
| Source | Placement cell / careers page / LinkedIn / Naukri / referral |
| Link to job post | URL |
| Résumé version | v3-web |
| Date applied | 2026-12-02 |
| Status | Applied → OA → Interview → Offer / Rejected |
| Next action and date | Follow up on 9 Dec |
| Contact | Name of referrer or recruiter, if any |
| Notes | Skills asked for; interview questions you got |

The scorecard target is a tracker with **at least 10 targeted opportunities**. "Targeted" means you read the job
description, checked that you fit, and tailored your résumé where needed.

Review the tracker twice a week. After every interview, add the questions you were asked; patterns in those notes tell
you what to revise.

## Internship and off-campus search strategy

Campus placement is one route, not the only one.

**Where to look**

- **Company careers pages** directly: the most reliable source for genuine openings.
- **LinkedIn and Naukri** job search with alerts for your target roles and cities.
- **Referrals** from alumni, seniors and people you meet at events. Ask politely, after you have checked the role fits,
  and share your résumé and the job link.
- **Hackathons, open-source contributions and coding contests**, which create visible evidence and contacts.

**Writing to a referrer or recruiter** (short and specific):

```text
Hi <name>, I'm a final-year <branch> student at <college>. I saw the <role> opening
(<link>). I recently built <one-line capstone description with live link>, where I owned
<your feature>. Would you be open to referring me, or advising whether my profile fits?
Résumé attached. Thank you for your time.
```

**Protect yourself from job scams.** Genuine employers do not ask candidates to pay for training, "registration",
equipment or interview slots, and do not hire without any interview. Check that the email domain matches the company,
and verify openings on the company's official careers page. If in doubt, ask your placement cell.

**Keep learning visibly.** Continue the DSA habit (the LeetCode 150 sheet), add one improvement to your capstone or a
new small project every few weeks, and post about it. Recruiters notice candidates who keep building after the
programme ends.

## The Placement Readiness Scorecard

You review the scorecard with your tutor this week and sign it off.

| Area | Target to be "placement ready" |
|---|---|
| DSA | 150+ problems solved (about 70 easy, 70 medium, 10 hard) across all patterns, logged in the tracker |
| Aptitude | 70% or more in the Final Placement Readiness Exam aptitude section |
| Technical | 60% or more in the technical MCQ section |
| Projects | 5 mini projects + capstone on GitHub; at least 3 deployed or demonstrable |
| Résumé | v3 approved by the tutor |
| Profiles | GitHub, LinkedIn, Naukri and portfolio checklists 100% complete |
| Interviews | Average mock interview score 3.5/5 or higher |
| Applications | Job application tracker set up with at least 10 targeted opportunities |

If you are below target in an area, agree a specific plan with your tutor: for example, "two medium problems a day for
three weeks" or "retake the aptitude mock in two weeks". The scorecard is a snapshot of readiness, not a final verdict.

## Programme close and certificates

Eligibility for a completion certificate requires attendance of 80% or more, all five mini projects submitted, and the
capstone presented. Your coordinator will confirm your status and the certificate process.

Before you leave:

- [ ] Every repository is public (or shared as your programme requires), pinned and documented.
- [ ] Demo videos are uploaded and linked.
- [ ] Your tracker, story bank and action plan are saved somewhere you will keep using.
- [ ] You have thanked your teammates and tutor, and connected with them on LinkedIn.

## Lab: final scorecard review

1. Upload résumé v3 for approval.
2. Complete every profile checklist; fix any broken links.
3. Set up your job application tracker with at least 10 targeted opportunities.
4. Review the scorecard with your tutor; agree a plan for any area below target; sign off.
