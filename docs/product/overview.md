# Product Overview & Core Business Strategy

This document articulates the business model, pedagogical positioning, and value proposition of RMS Careers.

---

## 1. Core Brand & Positioning

### Brand Message:
> **"Your Degree Gets You Started. Your Skills Get You Hired."**

RMS Careers helps B.Tech engineering students build technical skills, production projects, career confidence, and job readiness through structured curricula, live mentorship, and practical preparation.

### Fundamental Business Model:
RMS Careers is **NOT** a direct-to-consumer course marketplace. It does not sell one-off video courses or provide public shopping carts.
- **Institutional / B2B Model**: RMS Careers partners directly with engineering colleges, universities, and academic departments through formal MoUs.
- **Delivery**: Cohort-based training delivered directly to college student batches (e.g. 2nd, 3rd, and 4th year B.Tech students across CSE, IT, ECE, EEE, Mechanical, Civil).
- **Primary Conversion**: The corporate website is designed to convert college leadership (Principals, Deans, HODs, TPOs) via **"Partner With RMS"** conversations.

---

## 2. Six Canonical Service Pillars

RMS Careers delivers an integrated technical preparation ecosystem:

1. **Technical Skills**: Pattern-oriented training in Python, Java, DSA, Web Development (MERN), DBMS & SQL, Testing/QA, and SAP Fundamentals.
2. **Projects & Portfolio**: Production-grade full-stack architectures, collaborative Git workflows, relational schema design, and live deployment.
3. **Career Preparation**: ATS-compliant technical resumes, LinkedIn positioning, professional communication, and quantitative/logical aptitude.
4. **Job Preparation**: Company-specific preparation archives, 1-on-1 technical mock interview simulations, and placement assistance.
5. **Curated Resources**: Lecture slide decks, high-yield DSA roadmaps, interview cheatsheets, and revision guides.
6. **Personalized Guidance**: Dedicated software practitioner tutors, interactive Q&A sessions, progress tracking dashboards, and cohort community.

---

## 3. Flagship Offering: 1-Month Career Readiness Program

An intensive, 4-week cohort curriculum delivered in collaboration with partner engineering colleges:
- **Week 1**: Technical Diagnostics & Core Foundations (Algorithmic patterns, complexity analysis).
- **Week 2**: Project Architecture & Practical Implementation (Full-stack coding, database modeling, Git collaboration).
- **Week 3**: Portfolio Optimization & Aptitude Readiness (ATS resume reviews, quantitative problem-solving).
- **Week 4**: Mock Technical Interviews & Placement Drills (Live 1-on-1 mock interviews, readiness scorecards).

---

## 4. Student Experience Distinction

- **Guest Student**: Explores free starter DSA sheets (`/learn/dsa`) and CS roadmaps without login. Progress is saved locally in browser storage.
- **Enrolled Student**: Gains access to the authenticated Student Portal (`student.rms-careers.com`) with dedicated tutor feedback, verified assignments, XP streaks, and campus cohort leaderboards.
